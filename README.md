# AquaTwin-Drip Digital Twin for controlling drip irrigation.
The Challenge of Irrigation: From Empirical Decision-Making to Precision Management. In sub-Saharan Africa, and particularly in Benin, irrigation decisions are often based on experience, leading to suboptimal water use. This situation is exacerbated by high climate variability and often sandy soils (ferrallitic, lixisols) with low water-holding capacity.
Drip irrigation is a solution, but its effectiveness depends on the dynamics of the “moisture bulb,” a localized zone of moisture that traditional methods cannot manage with precision. There is a need for an explainable, robust, and data-efficient decision-support tool.
Our vision is an Agricultural Digital Twin: a framework that integrates process physics (soil), agronomic knowledge (plant), and available data to create an explainable and useful model.
